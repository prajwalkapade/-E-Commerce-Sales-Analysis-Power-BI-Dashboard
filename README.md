📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

I recently built an interactive dashboard to analyze sales performance, profit trends, customer behavior, and product categories in an e-commerce business.

📊 Dashboard Highlights

-Revenue & Profit Overview: Total Sales (438K), Profit (37K), and Quantity Sold (5.6K) at a glance.
-State-wise Performance: Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh lead in total sales contribution.
-Category Mix: Clothing dominates sales volume, followed by Electronics and Furniture.
-Monthly Profit Trend: Clear seasonality with strong gains in Q1 and Q4, and dips during mid-year.
-Customer Analysis: Top customers driving significant portions of revenue.
-Payment Modes: COD and UPI are the most preferred, showing digital adoption trends.
-Sub-Category Profitability: Printers and Bookcases generate the highest profits.

🧩 Techniques Used
📌 Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)
📌 Data Modeling & DAX Measures
📌 Time-Series Analysis
📌 Category & Customer Segmentation
📌 KPI Cards & Interactive Slicers (Quarter, State, Category)

🎯 Key Takeaway
This dashboard shows how sales data can uncover seasonal demand, profitable product segments, and customer payment preferences—helping businesses optimize inventory, marketing, and pricing strategies.
